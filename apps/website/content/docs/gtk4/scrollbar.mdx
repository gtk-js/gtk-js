## Behavior

- Most apps should use `GtkScrolledWindow` instead, whose scrollbars are these.
- Pressing the slider grabs it; a click in the trough centers the slider on the pointer.
- Shift+click or a middle click pages towards the pointer while held; the wheel moves by pages.

[GTK4 Documentation →](https://docs.gtk.org/gtk4/class.Scrollbar.html)
