## Behavior

- On the web, the window takes the size of its content by default. Use `style` or `className` to control sizing.
- The `.csd` class enables client-side decorations (border-radius, shadow).
- The `maximized`, `fullscreen` and `tiled` props add those classes: square corners, and no shadow when maximized or fullscreen. The maximize button shows the restore icon while `maximized`.
- The child gets the whole area below the titlebar; its `halign`/`valign` place it. A window has one child: put several in a `GtkBox`.
- `title` is shown by a `GtkHeaderBar` without a title widget.
- The window controls and title bars of header bars inside it call `onClose`, `onMinimize`, `onMaximize` and `onDrag`: a title bar starts a move once dragged past the drag threshold, toggles maximization on a double click, and opens a window menu on a right click.
