## Behavior

- Every button shows an icon and a label: side by side with `policy="wide"`, the icon above the label with `policy="narrow"`. A page without `iconName` shows the image-missing icon.
- Without `activePageName` the switcher keeps its own selection, starting on the first visible page.
- Tab focuses the selected button, and the arrow keys move between buttons. Clicking a button doesn't take the keyboard focus.
- Holding a drag over a button for half a second selects its page.

## AdwViewSwitcherBar

A bar for the bottom of a window, holding a narrow view switcher. It's shown when `reveal` is set and there are at least two visible pages.
