## Behavior

- Escape or the header bar's close button close it. When `canClose` is false, they call `onCloseAttempt` instead.
- `presentationMode="auto"` shows a bottom sheet while the window (or viewport) is at most 450px wide or 360px tall. In bottom-sheet mode, clicking the dimmed area also closes it.
- While it's open, Tab and the arrow keys move the focus within the dialog, and the page behind it doesn't scroll.
