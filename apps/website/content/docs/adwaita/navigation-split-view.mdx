## Behavior

- Uncollapsed, the sidebar takes `sidebarWidthFraction` of the width, between `minSidebarWidth` and `maxSidebarWidth`.
- Collapsed, the panes become the pages of a navigation view: the sidebar is the root page and the content is pushed over it, with a back button, Escape and Alt+← to go back. With `sidebarPosition="end"` it's the other way around.
- `useNavigation().push(content.tag)` shows the content, collapsed or not. `showContent` is kept across collapsing, and `onShowContentChanged` reports changes.
- Collapsing an uncollapsed view keeps the pages' state and focus.
- Uncollapsed, header bars hide the window buttons facing the other pane.
- Collapse it with an [AdwBreakpointBin](/docs/adwaita/breakpoint-bin), as in the preview.
