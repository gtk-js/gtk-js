## Behavior

- Gives the child the full width up to `tighteningThreshold`, then eases it towards `maximumSize`, centered.
- Puts `.small`, `.medium`, or `.large` CSS classes on the child based on its size relative to `tighteningThreshold` and `maximumSize`.
- Never makes the child smaller than its minimum size, even above `maximumSize`.
