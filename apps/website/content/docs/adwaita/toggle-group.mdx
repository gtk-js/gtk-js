Each toggle shows a label, an icon (name it with a `tooltip`), both, or a custom `child`. Without `active` or `activeName` the group keeps its own active toggle, starting with the first enabled one.

## CSS Node Tree

```
toggle-group[.horizontal|.vertical]
├── toggle.toggle[.text-button|.image-button|.image-text-button]
│   └── label | image | buttoncontent
├── separator.horizontal|.vertical[.hidden]
└── toggle.toggle
    └── ...
```

The separators take the group's orientation. A separator next to a hovered, checked or keyboard-focused toggle gets `.hidden`.

## Homogeneous

With `homogeneous`, every toggle is as wide as the widest one.

<PreviewFile file="content/docs/adwaita/examples/toggle-group-homogeneous.tsx">
  <HomogeneousExample />
</PreviewFile>

The toggles share any extra width the group gets. With `canShrink` (the default) their labels ellipsize when the group is narrower than its contents.

## Keyboard

- **Tab** — focuses the active toggle, and leaves the group from any toggle
- **Arrow keys** — move the focus between the toggles without activating them
- **Space / Enter** — activates the focused toggle (adds `.keyboard-activating` while it does)
