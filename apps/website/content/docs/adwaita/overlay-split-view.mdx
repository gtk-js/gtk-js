## CSS Node Tree

```
overlay-split-view
├── dimming.left|.right
├── shadow.left|.right
├── border.left|.right
├── widget[.content-pane]
│   ╰── [content]
├── widget
╰── widget[.sidebar-pane|.background][.end]
    ╰── [sidebar]
```

Not collapsed, the panes have the `.content-pane` and `.sidebar-pane` style
classes. Collapsed, the content pane has none and the sidebar pane has
`.background`. `dimming`, `shadow` and `border` (along the sidebar's edge) and
the empty `widget` (which takes the clicks that hide the sidebar) are only
shown while the sidebar overlays the content. The sidebar pane gets `.end`
when `sidebarPosition` is `"end"`.

## Behavior

- Not collapsed, sidebar and content sit side by side. The sidebar takes
  `sidebarWidthFraction` of the width, between `minSidebarWidth` and
  `maxSidebarWidth`, and never less than its child's minimum width.
- Collapsed, the sidebar overlays the dimmed content, `maxSidebarWidth` wide
  when there's room.
- Showing and hiding the sidebar slides it in and out. Both panes stay mounted,
  so their state survives collapsing and hiding.
- Collapsing hides the sidebar and uncollapsing shows it, reported through
  `onShowSidebarChanged`, unless `pinSidebar` is set. Collapse it on narrow
  widths with an `AdwBreakpointBin` breakpoint (`max-width: 400sp`).
- While the sidebar overlays the content, a click on the content or Escape
  hides it, and the content can't take the focus. Hiding the sidebar moves the
  focus out of it; showing it over the content moves the focus into it.
- On touchscreens, a swipe from the sidebar's edge shows it and a swipe on the
  sidebar hides it (`enableShowGesture`, `enableHideGesture`).
