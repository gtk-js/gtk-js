## Behavior

- Pages are pushed and popped from a navigation stack. Call `useNavigation()` inside a page for `push` and `pop`; pushing a tag the view doesn't have pushes it onto the enclosing navigation view.
- An `AdwHeaderBar` in a page shows the page's title, and a back button when there is a page to go back to. Right-click, long press, Shift+F10 or Menu on the back button opens a menu of the pages below.
- Pages stay mounted once shown, so they keep their state when popped and pushed again.
- A page with `canPop: false` has no back button, and the shortcuts and gestures below don't pop it.

## Keyboard and Gestures

- **Escape** — Pops the visible page (unless `popOnEscape` is `false`)
- **Alt+←** and the back mouse button — Pop the visible page
- **Swipe right** on a touchscreen, or **scroll right** on a touchpad — Pops the visible page, following the fingers

In right-to-left text, the arrow key and the gestures are mirrored.
