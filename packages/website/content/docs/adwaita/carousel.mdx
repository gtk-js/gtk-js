## Behavior

- Each child is a page. Pages are as wide as the widest child, or as the carousel when a child sets `hexpand` (`vexpand` when vertical), and the current page is centered.
- Drag pages with the mouse or touch, or swipe with a touchpad: the pages follow, and a swipe goes to the next page at most unless `allowLongSwipes` is set. A quick flick goes on to the next page.
- A mouse wheel notch goes to the next or previous page. Set `allowScrollWheel={false}` to turn it off, or `allowMouseDrag={false}` to leave dragging to touch.
- While something in the carousel has the focus, <kbd>←</kbd> and <kbd>→</kbd> (<kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>↓</kbd> when vertical) and <kbd>Page Up</kbd> and <kbd>Page Down</kbd> go to the previous or next page, <kbd>Home</kbd> and <kbd>End</kbd> to the first or last.
- `page` changes the page from code, with an animation, and `onPageChanged` reports the page once it settles. With `interactive={false}`, only `page` changes it: the user can't.
- `onPositionChanged` reports the scroll position as it moves, in pages. Pass it with the page count to `AdwCarouselIndicatorDots` or `AdwCarouselIndicatorLines`, which follow it. As in libadwaita, the indicators only show the position; they can't be clicked.
