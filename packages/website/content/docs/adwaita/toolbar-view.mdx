## Behavior

- The bars are drawn over the content: `extendContentToTopEdge` / `extendContentToBottomEdge` give the content the area under them.
- `revealTopBars` / `revealBottomBars` slide the bars in and out; hidden bars stay mounted and keep their state.
- Several bars on one side get `.collapse-spacing`, which makes header bars thinner.
- Empty space in the bars moves the window, like a header bar.
