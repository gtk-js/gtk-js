## Keyboard

- **Arrow keys** — move the slider by a step (Up decreases a vertical scale), stopping at marks
- **Page Up / Page Down**, **Ctrl + Arrow** — move by a page (`pageStep`)
- **+ / −** — increase / decrease by a step (with **Ctrl**, by a page)
- **Home / End** — jump to min / max

## Pointer

- **Click** the trough — jump there; **Shift + click** or **middle click** — page towards it; **right click** — scroll towards that end
- **Drag** the slider; **Shift** (or a long press) fine-tunes, **Escape** stops the drag
- **Scroll wheel** — move by a page
