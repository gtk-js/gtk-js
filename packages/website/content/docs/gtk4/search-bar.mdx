## Behavior

- Slides in when `searchModeEnabled` turns true and focuses its `GtkSearchEntry`; hiding it clears the entry.
- Escape in the entry and the close button hide it (`onSearchModeChanged(false)`). Without `searchModeEnabled` the bar keeps its own state.
- With `keyCaptureRef`, typing a printable key elsewhere in that element reveals the bar and types the key into the entry.

[GTK4 Documentation →](https://docs.gtk.org/gtk4/class.SearchBar.html)
