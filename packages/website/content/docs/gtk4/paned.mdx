## Behavior

- Drag the separator handle to resize the two panes. The position is clamped to the
  children's minimum sizes unless they can shrink (`shrinkStartChild`, `shrinkEndChild`),
  and `resizeStartChild`/`resizeEndChild` pick which child grows when the paned does.
- **F8** from a child focuses the handle: the arrow keys move it by 1px (with
  **Ctrl**, or **Page Up**/**Page Down**, by 75px), **Home**/**End**
  to the ends, **Enter** keeps the position and **Escape** restores it.
- `wideHandle` makes the separator easier to grab.
