## Behavior

- Pass the model to a `GtkMenuButton` as `menuModel`, or a `GtkPopoverMenu` as its `popover`.
- When the menu opens, its first item takes the focus. Up/Down and Tab move between the items, Enter or Space activates one, and Escape closes the menu.
- An underscore in a label marks its mnemonic: in the open menu, the key alone activates the item. The underline shows once the keyboard moves through the menu.
- An item's `accel` activates it while the menu is open. Browsers keep some shortcuts, such as Ctrl+N, for themselves.
- A menu taller than the window scrolls.
