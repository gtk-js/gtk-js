## Behavior

- Use `GtkNotebookPage` children to declare each tab and its content.
- Tabs can be positioned at top, bottom, left, or right via `tabPos`.
- Every page stays mounted, so pages keep their state, and the notebook is as large as its largest page.
- The notebook itself takes the keyboard focus for its tabs: the arrow keys, Home and End switch pages, Down moves into the page and Up comes back, and Ctrl+PageUp/PageDown switch pages from anywhere inside.
- With `scrollable`, tabs that don't fit are hidden and arrow buttons switch pages.
- A `reorderable` page can be dragged, or moved with Alt+arrow keys, Alt+Home and Alt+End; `onPageReordered` reports the new position and the app reorders its pages.
